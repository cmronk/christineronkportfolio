## The problem

The Trinity Fellowship Association of Churches is a network of pastors and churches that offers coaching groups, roundtables, strategic planning, and resources. Their old website depended on an outside developer, so every new event meant reaching out and waiting for someone else to make the change.

## How I approached it

I built the entire site in Rock RMS around the content the team updates most:

- **Events** use Rock's built-in event calendar. I designed the event cards, so every event looks consistent and the team only enters the details in Rock.
- **Coaching groups** come from Rock groups, so the site stays in sync with how the team already manages them.
- **Resources and other content** live in content channels the team can edit directly.

Once it launched, I trained the team to manage it themselves.

## The redesign

In 2026 I redesigned the site with a cleaner, more modern look.

## The result

The TFAC team publishes their own events and updates their own content, with no developer required.
