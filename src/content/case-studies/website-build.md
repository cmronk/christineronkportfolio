## The problem

The Trinity Fellowship Association of Churches is a network of pastors and churches that offers coaching groups, roundtables, strategic planning, and resources. Their old website depended on an outside developer: every new event meant reaching out and waiting for someone else to make the change. [Add anything else that wasn't working.]

My manager wasn't sure how to approach the rebuild, so I took it on.

## How I approached it

I built the entire site in Rock RMS and designed it around the content the team updates most:

- **Events** use Rock's built-in event calendar. I designed how they display on the site, including the event cards, so every event looks consistent and the team only enters the details in Rock.
- **Coaching groups** come from Rock groups, so the site stays in sync with how the team already manages them.
- **Resources and other content** live in content channels the team can edit directly.

Once it launched, I trained the team on how to manage it themselves.
